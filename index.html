About

Alin Alex Calugaru is a Romanian author and independent creator based in France. His work explores human memory, emotion, identity, technology and the traces people leave behind.

Books & Research

THE SPARK CODE

A philosophical science-fiction and romantic novel exploring love, memory, identity and the possibility that something of us may persist beyond autobiographical memory.

THE LAST STEP. THE FIRST TRACE.

A work presenting the Affective Hash Persistence Hypothesis (AHPH), exploring whether affective traces could persist independently of autobiographical memory.
