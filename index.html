About the Author

Alin Alex Calugaru was born on November 22, 1978, in Târgu Jiu, Romania.

His travels across Europe and parts of Asia exposed him to different cultures, perspectives and stories, enriching his imagination and shaping his creative vision.

As an author, he explores a wide range of literary worlds, from science fiction and mystery to psychological fiction, romance, comedy, and stories for children.

His books invite readers to travel beyond the familiar, discover unexpected possibilities, experience powerful emotions, and sometimes question the very nature of reality.

For Alin Alex Calugaru, writing is more than storytelling. It is the art of creating worlds that continue to exist long after the final page has been turned.
